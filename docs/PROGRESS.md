(2026-09-12, commit `553ecfb`). The full objective remains active. Do not interpret working rendering,
passing validators or licence checks as validation of a complete anatomical atlas: no
anatomist has reviewed any mesh, landmark or registration.

Result: a pelvis-anchor offset shared by every level (hips + sacrum: x -0.15, y +0.86, z -3.23 mm, norm 3.35 mm) whose cause this calculation does not identify (registration translation, angular registration error or anchor segmentation; subtracting it removes one translational reference only, and 0.5 deg of angular error alone gives about 5 mm at 600 mm from the pelvis); relative to the pelvis the offset grows smoothly from 0.8 to 1.0 mm at the lowest lumbar-type bodies and the sacrum, to about 2 mm posterior through the thoracic spine (norm 1.7 to 2.7 mm), 2.5 to 4 mm at C4 to C7 and 4.8 to 5.0 mm at C1 to C3 (4 mm posterior, 2.7 mm inferior); the skull anchor moves 6.8 mm as placed with a 3.8 deg own fit. Per-instance own-fit rotations are below 0.5 deg except V05 (4.3 deg), V22 (1.8 deg) and V25 (1.3 deg), whose after-fit residuals (2.0 to 2.7 mm p95 against 0.7 to 1.0 mm elsewhere) are larger; the residual mixes segmentation differences with discretisation, sampling and fit limits and is not attributed. A heuristic discrepancy threshold is recorded per level, not an error bound and not a confidence level (it propagates no angular uncertainty, the models may share bias, and a per-axis range is not a Euclidean range): absolute = pelvis-anchor offset 3.35 mm + the largest per-component range across the three models (1.7 to 4.3 mm); relative = the largest disagreement between the three pelvis anchors about the anchor offset (0.76 mm) + the model range. Only C1 to C3 lie beyond both; being beyond marks a discrepancy to inspect and attributes nothing to posture. Ribs: 1.6 to 6.2 mm relative to the pelvis, largest at RL06, RL07, RL10 and RR06 (ribs are thin and the vote boundaries differ). Chain fits are flagged: the vertebra centroids are nearly collinear (second singular value below 15 % of the first on every region), so the rotation about the spine axis and the region translations are not physical and only the residuals are read (whole spine RMS 1.37 mm, thoracic 0.23 mm). What this does not separate: posture from registration (translation and angle) from segmentation, the pelvis-anchor offset included; nothing is corrected, no level is named, and nothing is anatomy. Codex audit of 38308be (2026-09-13, three P2, all applied the same day): a label that voted into two consensus instances (`vertebrae_L1` -> V20 and V21, the six-lumbar split) keeps every linked instance in `trunk_posture_context.linked_instance_ids` / `linked_levels`, figures from the linked instance nearest in z to the mesh centroid (validator checks the set, the choice and the list); the anchor offset is no longer called a registration difference; the sum is called a heuristic threshold. Consequence for stage 1: the fresh-CT trunk labels enter the cryosection frame as priors with this per-level measured discrepancy (about 2 mm relative to the pelvis through the thoracic and lumbar spine, about 5 mm at the upper cervical spine and skull, plus the 3.35 mm pelvis-anchor offset of unidentified cause); nothing is corrected from these figures, and any correction needs its own protocol (sensitivity to the registrations and models, fixed before use) after the alignment check against Denver's aligned slices.

## Known limitations

- Every registration is automatic. The same-donor CT fit is rigid and tight at the pelvis (p95 4.7 mm) but the CT sacrum differs from the Denver sacrum by 9.8 mm p95 (extent and coccyx), and the femora moved 1.6 to 3.0 deg at the hip between acquisitions; the CT trunk above the pelvis inherits any spinal posture difference between the fresh CT (on the table) and the frozen block, which is unmeasured.
- The two CT exams (vertex to thigh, thigh to feet) were stacked with a 1 mm continuity assumption; the measured in-plane shift at the junction (5.6 x 3.8 mm) is recorded, not corrected. Only exam 370 (trunk) enters the composite; the exam 371 legs are covered by Denver.
- TotalSegmentator labels are model output on a 1993 cadaver CT; boundaries, missing labels (prostate, kidney cysts, portal vein union) and label extents are unreviewed. Stray islands below 5 % of a label were removed and counted.
- HRA organs are placed by a six-proxy similarity (RMS 7.4 mm on bounding-box centres), which does not validate organ shape or position; HRA organs with the same term as a CT label are dropped, so the composite mixes one measured donor with a reference assembly.
- The composite has no VHF hands, forearm bones, patellae, tibiae, fibulae or feet from CT: those come from Denver (feet, patellae, tibiae, fibulae) or are missing (hands, radius, ulna). TCIA forearm bones are not composed.
- Landmarks are automatic geometric rules; slab centroids sit up to 6.6 mm off the surface. Manual landmark review is pending.
- CT tissue depots (TCIA) and CT muscle compartments (NLM autochthon) contain nonmanifold edges or self-intersections; they are preserved as labelled compartments, not repaired. Every consensus vertebra and rib mesh also carries a few self-intersecting pairs (median 6) from the same voxel-to-mesh pipeline.
- Composition 0.5 shipped between 2026-09-10 and 2026-09-12 with `not-assessed` QA fields in manifests and viewer, and without QA metadata on the consensus instances in the composite (commit `b0063fd`); corrected in `083589a` and `553ecfb`, with the validator now refusing that state.
- Plan B section 3.6 (agentic visual review of cryosections) is an optional experiment whose adoption and abandonment criteria are incremental (errors removed against current controls, false alarms, cost, pre-registered thresholds); the verifier is a second segmenter with its own proposal, not an independent validator, and regions without a Denver reference limit the conclusions instead of cancelling the pilot.
- `Toes` (TCIA) has outlying geometry above the head; it is flagged in `generated/anatomy-qa.json` and excluded from the composite.
- Source-local labels resolve to UBERON/FMA by exact label or synonym with OLS evidence, but no anatomist has confirmed the equivalences; 168 entries remain unresolved.
- Upstream npm installation reports 11 vulnerabilities. Dependency remediation has not yet been evaluated.
